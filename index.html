Our Technology

NaMo Technologies combines artificial intelligence, database engineering, and information delivery into a layered technical architecture. Each service runs on the same foundation: structured data ingestion, validated retrieval, AI-supported processing, and controlled output delivery.

Artificial Intelligence & Large Language Models

Our AI layer is built around state-of-the-art large language models (LLMs) used for summarization, classification, and draft generation. We combine task-specific prompting, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and structured output schemas to ensure that every generated result is grounded in verifiable source data. For chatbot use cases, the LLM operates strictly on retrieved context from the customer's defined databases, significantly reducing hallucination risk.

Model outputs are validated through multi-stage pipelines: automated schema validation, confidence scoring, and human-in-the-loop review by our sector experts to enforce tone, scope, and compliance requirements.

Database & Data Engineering

Reliable automation depends on reliable data. Our data engineering layer covers ingestion from heterogeneous sources, normalization into consistent schemas, deduplication, and entity resolution across formats. We implement data lineage tracking so that every output can be traced back to its source records.

For chatbot deployments, the knowledge base is versioned and continuously validated, so that answers always reflect the current state of approved company data rather than outdated information.

Information Delivery & Monitoring Infrastructure

Collecting and processing information is only useful if it reaches the right people at the right time. Our delivery layer includes scheduled crawlers, event-driven alerting, report generation pipelines, and multi-channel distribution. Alerts are deduplicated and enriched with context before delivery, so recipients receive actionable information rather than raw data.
